AFTER REPAIR PROCEDURE [SJ6A-EL]

id050208800400

Caution

• After repairing a malfunction, perform this procedure to verify that the malfunction has been corrected. • When this procedure is carried out, be sure to drive the vehicle at lawful speed and pay attention to the other vehicles.

  1. Connect the M-MDS to the DLC-2.

  1. After the vehicle is identified, select the following items from the initial screen of the M-MDS.

• When using the IDS (laptop PC)

  1. Select the "Toolbox" tab. 2. Select "Self Test". 3. Select "Modules". 4. Select "TCM".

• When using the PDS (Pocket PC)

  1. Select "Module Tests". 2. Select "TCM". 3. Select "Self Test".

  2. Verify the DTC according to the directions on the screen.

  3. Press the clear button on the DTC screen to clear the DTC.

  4. Perform the following trouble code inspections to ensure that the DTC has been resolved:

DTC No.

inspection

P0601, P0603, P0604, P0712, P0713, P0717, P0722, P0882, P0883, P0961, P0962, P0963, P0969, P0970, P0971, P0973, P0974, P0976, P0977, P0979, P0980, P0982, P0983, P0985, P0986, P2719, P2720, P2721, P2762, P2763, P2764

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine and AT. iii. Drive the vehicle in D range and make sure that the gears shift smoothly from 1GR to 6GR. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

P0707

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Depress the brake pedal, and shift the selector lever from P to D for 2 s or more. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

P0708

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Depress the brake pedal, and shift the selector lever from P to D for 2 s or more. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Start the engine. vii. Depress the brake pedal, and shift the selector lever from P to D for 2 s or more. viii. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. ix. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. x. Go to Step 6.

P0711

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Drive the vehicle in D range for 10 min or more. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

P0751, P0752, P0756, P0757, P0761, P0762, P0766, P0781, P0813

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine and AT. iii. Drive the vehicle in D range and make sure that the gears shift smoothly from 1GR to 6GR. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Start the engine. vii. Drive the vehicle in D range and make sure that the gears shift smoothly from 1GR to 6GR. viii. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. ix. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. x. Go to Step 6.

P0819

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Drive the vehicle in M range, and shift the selector lever (operate up and down switches) between 1GR to 6GR for 10 s or more. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

P0826

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Drive the vehicle in M range, and shift the steering shift switch (operate up and down switches) between 1GR to 6GR for 10 s or more. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

P2757, P2758

i. Start the engine. ii. Warm up the engine to normal operating temperature. iii. Drive the vehicle in D range and make sure that the gears shift smoothly from 1GR to 6GR and TCC is operated. iv. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle. v. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position. vi. Go to Step 6.

  1. Gradually slow down and stop the vehicle.

  2. Make sure that no DTCs occur.
