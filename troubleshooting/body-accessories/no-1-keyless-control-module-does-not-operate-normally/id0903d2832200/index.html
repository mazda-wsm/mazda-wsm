NO.1 KEYLESS CONTROL MODULE DOES NOT OPERATE NORMALLY

id0903d2832200

Applied VIN (assumed):

• JMZ NC1882*# 100001-103859

• JMZ NC18820# 100001-103859

• JMZ NC18F2*# 100001-103859

• JMZ NC18F20# 100001-103859

• JMZ NC18F6*# 100001-103859

• JMZ NC18F60# 100001-103859

• JM0 NC30F100 100001-100545

1

One or more on-board diagnostic functions inoperative

DESCRIPTION

• Malfunction in trunk lid opener system, horn system, hazard warning light system, or door lock linkage system

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Malfunction in keyless control module power supply - Open circuit in IG1 and/or B+ power supply circuit - Open or short circuit in ground circuit - Keyless control module malfunction • Malfunction in keyless switch related circuit - Keyless switch malfunction • Malfunction in door switch signal circuit - Open or short circuit in wiring between keyless control module and door switch - Door switch malfunction • Malfunction in door lock/unlock signal circuit - Door lock actuator malfunction - Open or short circuit in wiring between keyless control module and door lock actuator • Malfunction in turn light signal circuit - Flasher unit malfunction - Open or short circuit in wiring between flasher unit and each turn signal light

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1

INSPECT FOR MALFUNCTION IN KEYLESS UNIT POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT OR ELSEWHERE • Did any of the following operate during the malfunction diagnosis? - All doors lock/unlock. - Hazard warning light flashes.

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Inspect the connection of the keyless control module connectors, and then go to Step 4.

2

INSPECT FOR MALFUNCTION IN KEYLESS SWITCH OR ELSEWHERE • Did the following operate during the malfunction diagnosis? - All doors lock/unlock.

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Go to step 7.

3

INSPECT FOR MALFUNCTION IN DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR OR ELSEWHERE • Did the following operate during the malfunction diagnosis? - Hazard warning light flashes.

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Go to step 8.

4

INSPECT KEYLESS UNIT POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT • Are the keyless control module power supply and voltage normal? - IG1 signal (terminal 3B) - B+ signal (terminal 1E, 1F)

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

• Inspect for a burnt fuse. • Inspect the power supply system wiring harness for an open or short circuit.

5

INSPECT KEYLESS UNIT GROUND CIRCUIT • Is the keyless control module ground voltage normal? - 0V (terminal 1B, 3C)

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Inspect the ground system wiring harness for an open circuit.

6

INSPECT LATCH SWITCH SIGNAL VOLTAGE •  Are the wiring harnesses between the keyless control module and each door switch normal? - Inspect each terminal (4O and 4T) under the following conditions. • Door closed: B+ • Door open: 2.0V or less

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

• Inspect the door switch. • Inspect the door switch system wiring harness for a short circuit.

7

INSPECT KEYLESS SWITCH • Is the keyless switch normal?

Yes

Replace the keyless control module.

No

Replace the keyless switch.

8

INSPECT DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR OPERATION SIGNAL • Is the signal voltage transmitted to the door lock actuator normal? • Does the signal voltage transmitted to the door lock actuator change as follows when the malfunction diagnosis inspection procedure is performed. - All doors locked: Cycles 1.0 V or less →B+→1.0 V or less (terminal 1A) - All doors unlocked: Cycles B+→1.0 V or less→B+ (terminal 1C)

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

• Inspect the wiring harness between the keyless control module and the door lock actuator for an open or short circuit. • Inspect the door lock actuator.

9

INSPECT TURN LIGHT SIGNAL VOLTAGE • Is the signal voltage transmitted to each turn light normal? • Does the signal voltage transmitted to each turn light change as follows when the malfunction diagnosis inspection procedure is performed. - Alternates between 1.0 or less and B+ (terminal 4N)

Yes

Verify the malfunction symptom again.

No

• Inspect the wiring harnesses between the keyless entry module and each turn light for a short circuit. • Inspect each turn light.
