NO.2 AUTO LEVELING DOES NOT ADJUST OPTICAL AXIS OF THE HEADLIGHTS FOR VEHICLE POSTURE

id0903d0930000

2

Auto leveling does not adjust optical axis of the headlights for vehicle posture

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Headlight leveling actuator the malfunction • Auto leveling control module the malfunction • Front and/or rear leveling sensor the malfunction • Improper installation of front and/or rear auto leveling sensor • Open or short circuit in wiring between auto leveling control module terminal W and instrument cluster terminal 2O

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1

VERIFY AUTO LEVELING WARNING LIGHT ILLUMINATION • Turn the ignition switch to ON position. • Verify that the auto leveling warning light condition. • Does the auto leveling warning light illuminate from turning the ignition switch to ON 3 seconds after?

Yes

Perform the symptom "No.1 Auto leveling warning light illuminates when the ignition switch to ON position" troubleshooting procedure.

No

Go to the next step.

2

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN AUTO LEVELING SENSOR OR ELSEWHERE • Is the malfunction appeared both side?

Yes

Go to Step 5.

No

Go to the next step.

3

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN WIRING HARNESS BETWEEN AUTO LEVELING CONTROL MODULE AND HEADLIGHT LEVELING ACTUATOR • Inspect for open or short circuit in wiring harness at auto leveling function does not operate. Right-side: - Auto leveling control module terminal E-front combination light (right-side) terminal G - Auto leveling control module terminal B-front combination light (right-side) terminal F - Auto leveling control module terminal T-front combination light (right-side) terminal H Left-side: - Auto leveling control module terminal G-front combination light (left-side) terminal G - Auto leveling control module terminal B-front combination light (left-side) terminal F - Auto leveling control module terminal T-front combination light (left-side) terminal H • Is open or short circuit detected?

Yes

Repair or replace for open or short circuit.

No

Go to the next step.

4

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN HEADLIGHT LEVELING ACTUATOR OR ELSEWHERE • Replace the suspected side front combination light. (See FRONT COMBINATION LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.) • Does the auto leveling adjust optical axis of the headlight for vehicle posture?

Yes

Troubleshooting completed. (The malfunction is in headlight leveling actuator.)

No

Replace the auto leveling control module. (See AUTO LEVELING CONTROL MODULE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION)

5

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN WIRING HARNESS BETWEEN AUTO LEVELING CONTROL MODULE AND HEADLIGHT LEVELING ACTUATOR • Inspect for open or short circuit in wiring harness. Right-side: - Auto leveling control module terminal E-front combination light (right-side) terminal G - Auto leveling control module terminal B-front combination light (right-side) terminal F Left-side: - Auto leveling control module terminal G-front combination light (left-side) terminal G - Auto leveling control module terminal B-front combination light (left-side) terminal F • Is open or short circuit detected?

Yes

Repair or replace for open or short circuit.

No

Go to the next step.

6

INSPECT FRONT AND REAR AUTO LEVELING SENSOR INSTALLATION • Inspect front and rear auto leveling sensor installation (bend or damaged of bracket, or dislocated link). • Are front and rear auto leveling sensor installed properly?

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Repair or replace the malfunctioning part, or install properly.

7

INSPECT FRONT AUTO LEVELING SENSOR • Inspect the front auto leveling sensor. (See FRONT AUTO LEVELING SENSOR INSPECTION.) • Is the front auto leveling sensor normal?

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Replace the front auto leveling sensor. (See FRONT AUTO LEVELING SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

8

INSPECT REAR AUTO LEVELING SENSOR • Inspect the rear auto leveling sensor. (See REAR AUTO LEVELING SENSOR INSPECTION.) • Is the rear auto leveling sensor normal?

Yes

Go to the next step.

No

Replace the rear auto leveling sensor. (See REAR AUTO LEVELING SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

9

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN VEHICLE SPEED SIGNAL LINE OR ELSEWHERE • Turn the ignition switch to OFF. • Disconnect the auto leveling control module connector. • Turn the ignition switch to ON position. • Measure the voltage between auto leveling control module harness side connector terminal W and GND. • Is the voltage B+?

Yes

Repair or replace for short to power supply.

No

Go to the next step.

10

INSPECT IF THE MALFUNCTION IS IN VEHICLE SPEED SIGNAL LINE OR ELSEWHERE • Turn the ignition switch to OFF. • Disconnect auto leveling control module and instrument cluster connectors. • Verify that the continuity for following: - Auto leveling control module terminal W-GND (normal condition: not continuity) - Auto leveling control module terminal W-instrument cluster terminal 2O (normal condition: continuity) • Are there continuity.

Yes

Replace the auto leveling control module. (See AUTO LEVELING CONTROL MODULE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

No

Repair or replace for open or short circuit.
